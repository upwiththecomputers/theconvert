# The Convert

A monotone reading room for the texts of Saint Augustine of Hippo.

Portraits of the saint are digitized into black ink dots on cream paper.
The cybernetic gesture is that conversion — sampling a painting into
signal — not circuitry. The words are public-domain English (Pusey, Dods,
Shaw, Haddan, Starbuck).

Live source: [github.com/upwiththecomputers/theconvert](https://github.com/upwiththecomputers/theconvert)

## Works

- Confessions
- The City of God
- On Christian Doctrine
- Soliloquies
- Enchiridion
- On the Trinity

## Plates

Public-domain paintings, reduced to hexagonal stipple:

- Philippe de Champaigne, *Saint Augustine*
- Gerard Seghers, *Saint Augustine*
- Sandro Botticelli, *Saint Augustine in His Study*
- Benozzo Gozzoli, *Saint Augustine Reading the Epistle of St Paul*
